Free partner dancing is a dance between two people that is not limited by any particular style or technique. It may include contact improvisation, swing, classical dance, learned figures, as well as movements that naturally arise between the two of you here and now. All forms are welcome.

Above all, it is a dialogue through movement – it teaches you to perceive the other person, respond to them and at the same time remain in contact with yourself. It teaches you what it means to lead and also what it means to surrender to being led.

It is a fantastic means for meditation in movement and for being carried into pure presence. It trains both attention and the body, and it can be deeply fulfilling and nourishing for body and soul.
