Dearmouring supports the gradual laying down of layers that once protected you, but may now prevent the experience of closeness, joy or fullness in the body. It is not about pushing through force, but about patiently listening to what is ready to soften.

The session can open more space for breath, sensitivity, groundedness and awareness of your own needs. The pace always remains rooted in safety, consent and mutual communication.
