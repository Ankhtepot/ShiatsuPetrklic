*Gentle, conscious work with places where protective tension, shame or suppressed emotion have been held in the body for a long time.*

- releasing tension
- respectful pacing
- deeper sensitivity

Dearmouring supports the gradual laying down of layers that once protected you but may now limit closeness, joy or fullness in the body. It is not about pushing through forcefully; it is about patient listening to what is ready to soften.

A session can open more space for breath, sensitivity, groundedness and awareness of your own needs. The pace always remains anchored in safety, consent and mutual communication.
