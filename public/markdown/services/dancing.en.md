*A space for spontaneous movement, self-contact, joy and the return of natural aliveness to the body.*

- authentic movement
- breath and rhythm
- body aliveness

Here, dancing is not about technique or how the movement looks from the outside. It is a path toward mobilising breath, emotion and vitality that are often muted beneath layers of daily control and fatigue.

It can take the form of free movement, guided exercises or gentle partnered exploration. The focus stays on authenticity, sensing and reconnecting with what naturally wants to move in the body.
