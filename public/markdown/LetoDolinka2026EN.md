Come to rest, connect, and play.

I often hear from people at workshops that it is not so important what they are coming for - what matters to them is meeting
friends and getting to know new people.

This gathering is inspired by that idea - to meet, maybe get to know new people, and reconnect with those you already know.

To share what interests us and what needs to be spoken out loud, to experiment, to try something new, and simply to play.

I can prepare a full program for the whole gathering, but I would be very happy if we created the program together. For example, we could try a new class,
or bring something for others that feels important to you.

What I bring is the art of touch, dance, and Shibari rope work.

What matters most to me is good company, depth, and playfulness. If you received an invitation, it is because
I feel you are in harmony with this idea 🙂 

I am not inviting you to a workshop or a tightly organized minute-by-minute event, but to a pleasant rest and gathering with like-minded people ❤

**Date:**<br>
The main event will take place from Friday <b>21 Aug</b> to Sunday <b>23 Aug</b>, but if you would like to arrive earlier or stay longer, that is no problem - just let me know.
I am planning to stay in Dolinka from the 20th until Monday the 24th, because I really like it there 🙂


**Place:**<br> 
We will be welcomed by the beautiful Dolinka center near Kozelnik and Banska Stiavnica.
